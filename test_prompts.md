# Day 3 research test prompts

1. **Market research:** Assess the current market for AI note-taking apps:
   target users, leading products, differentiators, and growth drivers.
2. **Technical comparison:** Compare LFP and NMC batteries for grid-scale
   storage across safety, cost, lifespan, and recyclability.
3. **Recent developments:** What are the most significant developments in
   open-source large language models in the past six months?
4. **Policy and regulation:** Summarize current regulatory approaches to
   generative AI transparency in the EU and United States.
5. **Case studies:** Find examples of small businesses using AI customer-support
   agents and summarize outcomes and pitfalls.
6. **Evidence review:** What does recent research say about how blue-light
   exposure affects sleep, and what are the main limitations?
